# Mettelo CV Template

A reusable CV structure for adapting your genuine experience to a specific role.

[← CV & résumé resources](README.md) · [CV development workflow](cv-development-workflow-edition-3.md) · [← Resource Hub](../../README.md)

> **Before using this template:** Replace every square-bracket placeholder with accurate information, delete any section or bullet that does not apply, and follow the employer's instructions on length, format and anonymisation. The wording below is a *blank template*, not a completed example or an achievement you should claim.

## Before you copy: three concrete writing decisions

| Your situation | What to prioritise | Why it helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First job or graduate** | Real academic projects, placements, volunteering, part-time work, relevant methods, and correctly labelled unfinished work | Employers can see applied capability even without many job titles |
| **Career changer** | Relevant transferable decisions, real project evidence and a truthful work chronology | Avoids disguising the previous career or inventing experience |
| **Experienced professional** | Most important recent responsibilities, technical judgement, evidence of delivery and credible outcomes | Helps the reviewer see impact and appropriate level quickly |

**Illustrative bullet transformation (fictional):** “Responsible for weekly data reports” could become “Checked weekly reporting totals against source extracts, resolved duplicate-record issues and documented changes for the reporting team.” The revised statement is only suitable if those activities really occurred.

**A five-minute test:** Read one bullet and ask who used the output, what was your responsibility, which method you used and what result is genuinely known. If the bullet lacks a fact, ask a question rather than requesting AI to add a percentage.

## Copyable CV template

Copy the Markdown below into your own file or text editor. If the application requires a Word or PDF file, format and export the completed CV in that format.

```markdown
# [YOUR NAME]

[City or region] | [Telephone] | [Professional email]  
[LinkedIn URL] | [Relevant portfolio URL]

## Professional profile

[Write approximately 40 to 60 words describing your relevant background, the needs you can address and two or three evidence-supported strengths for the target role.]

## Relevant skills

**[Skill group]:** [Relevant methods or capabilities supported by your experience]  
**[Tools or systems]:** [Relevant tools with an accurate level of experience]

## Employment history

### [Current or most relevant job title] | [Employer]

[Month YYYY] to [Present or Month YYYY] | [Location if relevant]

- [Specific action addressing a relevant problem, using a supported approach or tools, with an approved result; keep concise and specific.]
- [A distinct contribution showing appropriate scope, judgement or collaboration and its approved outcome; keep concise and specific.]
- [Another relevant achievement supported by your evidence bank; avoid repeating the same result.]

### [Previous relevant job title] | [Employer]

[Month YYYY] to [Present or Month YYYY] | [Location if relevant]

- [Specific action addressing a relevant problem, using a supported approach or tools, with an approved result; keep concise and specific.]
- [A distinct contribution showing appropriate scope, judgement or collaboration and its approved outcome; keep concise and specific.]
- [Another relevant achievement supported by your evidence bank; avoid repeating the same result.]

## Earlier employment

### [Earlier relevant job title] | [Employer]

[Month YYYY] to [Month YYYY]

- [Selected relevant contribution with a supported method and meaningful result; keep concise and specific.]

## Selected projects or volunteering

### [Project name] | [Accurate context and dates]

- [Your specific contribution and approved result; label academic, voluntary or independent work accurately and keep clear and relevant.]
- [A second distinct contribution if useful; remove the bullet or entire section when it adds no relevant evidence.]

## Education

[Qualification] | [Institution] | [Completion date or accurate current status]  
[Relevant additional qualification, if useful]

## Certifications and professional registration

[Exact certification or registration name] | [Issuing body] | [Date or current status]
```

## How to customise

1. **Confirm the vacancy requirements.** Follow the actual job description, selection criteria and submission rules rather than assuming one CV fits every role.
2. **Use approved evidence.** Only describe work, tools, qualifications, responsibilities and results that you can support and explain.
3. **Prioritise relevance.** Adjust the order and amount of detail in each role; remove irrelevant placeholders, bullets and optional sections.
4. **Check anonymisation.** Remove identifying information when the employer requires a name-blind CV.
5. **Review the final file.** Confirm your dates, links, spelling, readability and file format. Do not submit the placeholder template.

For a fuller preparation process, work through the [seven-step CV development workflow](cv-development-workflow-edition-3.md) before filling in this template.

### About the template

This is a Markdown adaptation of the supplied **Mettelo CV Template** (two-page Word document). Section names, placeholders and approximate profile guidance reflect the source. The source's suggested 15–25-word bullet length has been made **flexible**, because there is no universal mandatory bullet length and specific clarity matters more than a formula. Markdown does not preserve the original Word page layout; use an editable document application when you need precise pagination.

**AI and accuracy note:** AI writing tools can introduce unsupported achievements or inflate responsibilities. Review every claim yourself, protect confidential information and observe any employer restrictions on AI use.
