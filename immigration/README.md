## How to use the resources — five decisions

1. **Identify your real circumstances.** Technology recognition, creative professional recognition, research eligibility and an innovative UK venture are **different immigration routes**. An arts professional should not simply copy technology evidence categories.
2. **Open the detailed guide, not just the checklist.** Learn the official assessment and separate it from optional Mettelo study, networking and business-development activities.
3. **Check present official eligibility.** Review the exact government/assessor pages linked in the guide, especially route requirements, endorsements, current immigration permission, document limits and deadlines.
4. **Plan verifiable action.** Work with the guide's examples, outreach methods, 90-day or 24-month programmes and private evidence trackers. Don't purchase fabricated documentation or claim unachieved work.
5. **Make an informed application decision.** Confirm the right application route and relevant legal advice where personal immigration status, switching or expiry is involved.
