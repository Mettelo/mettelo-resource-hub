### How to prepare

1. Read the **introduction and eligibility** section of the pathway that fits your real work.
2. Check its **official requirements** and any specialist assessor rules.
3. If you need to develop stronger professional evidence, use the **24-month table as a menu of suggestions**, not a visa condition or guarantee.
4. Track genuine work, source documents and gaps using the linked checklist or [tracker guide](tracker-guide.md).
5. Verify current official rules before seeking endorsement or paying a fee.

## One current guide for each pathway

There is **one current guide per pathway**. Checklists and tracker instructions are supporting tools, not alternative guides.

## Status and limits

**All three enhanced guides remain drafts** pending final legal-content, link-by-link and rendering checks. Opportunity deadlines and eligibility can change. Original interactive Excel trackers are **not hosted in this repository**, although the [tracker instructions](tracker-guide.md) and Markdown checklists are readable.

These are educational materials, not regulated immigration advice. Use official Home Office and endorsing-body sources for decisions.
