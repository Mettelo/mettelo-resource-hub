# Arts and Culture — Markdown checklist

[← Tracker guide](tracker-guide.md) · [← Pathway guide](arts-and-culture.md) · [← Global Talent resources](README.md)

> **Source:** Adapted from the supplied original Mettelo Excel tracker. This is an editable *copying template*, not a live tracker, eligibility decision or legal checklist verified for today. **Do not put private evidence links or personal information in a public GitHub repository.** Create a private copy for your own progress.

**Statuses:** To do · Working on it · Ready · Not needed (only when genuinely inapplicable).

**For each item record privately:** target date, a link to your private evidence file, and notes about what is missing. Mark Ready only after checking the actual evidence and current official requirements.

## Source checklist items

- [ ] **1.** Confirm that general arts is the correct route for your discipline.
- [ ] **2.** Check talent or promise, professional history and required country coverage.
- [ ] **3.** Prepare a typed CV with professional career and education.
- [ ] **4.** Get a letter from an eligible UK arts organisation you worked with artistically.
- [ ] **5.** Get a letter from a second eligible expert arts organisation you worked with.
- [ ] **6.** Get a third eligible organisation or expert letter based on actual artistic work together.
- [ ] **7.** Check all three letters: content, signature, date, contacts and author credentials.
- [ ] **8.** Choose at least two evidence categories: critical media, prizes or professional appearances.
- [ ] **9.** If using media, collect at least two qualifying critical reviews and attribution.
- [ ] **10.** If using prizes, verify the qualifying award or permitted promise nomination.
- [ ] **11.** If using appearances, gather professional presentation and required source proof.
- [ ] **12.** Check at most ten single evidence pieces, within five years and two A4 pages each.
- [ ] **13.** If needed, obtain group contribution confirmations and required translations.
- [ ] **14.** Check the full current general arts requirements before submitting.
- [ ] **15.** Complete endorsement form, fee and required document handoff; save confirmation.
- [ ] **16.** Record endorsement decision and date; check the visa application deadline.
- [ ] **17.** Prepare passport, visa answers and any conditional visa documents.
- [ ] **18.** Complete visa payment, assigned identity check and supporting document steps.
## Personal progress log (keep private)

Use these columns in a private spreadsheet or notes file: **Date | What I did and the actual result | Evidence file link | My next step | Target date**. Each row should record one genuine action or outcome.

## Official sources

Confirm current requirements using the [GOV.UK Global Talent guidance](https://www.gov.uk/global-talent) and the relevant endorsement route. The source tracker may contain dated thresholds or procedural details and **does not replace live eligibility rules**.
