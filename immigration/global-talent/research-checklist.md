> **Source:** Adapted from the supplied original Mettelo Excel tracker. This is an editable *copying template*, not a live tracker, eligibility decision or legal checklist verified for today. **Do not put private evidence links or personal information in a public GitHub repository.** Create a private copy for your own progress.

**Statuses:** To do · Working on it · Ready · Not needed (only when genuinely inapplicable).

**For each item record privately:** target date, a link to your private evidence file, and notes about what is missing. Mark Ready only after checking the actual evidence and current official requirements.

## Choose one route first

The original workbook dynamically changes its first six checklist items when you select a route in cell B4. **Markdown has no dropdown or formulas**, so use only the relevant route below. Peer review talent and peer review promise are separate options in the Excel tracker.

### Appointment

- [ ] **1.** Verify the approved institution and actual qualifying appointment.
- [ ] **2.** Obtain the full job description showing the required research experience.
- [ ] **3.** Obtain the authorised HR guarantee with all required recruitment confirmations.
- [ ] **4.** Check that you accepted the offer and that position and department match.
- [ ] **5.** Verify all route conditions against the current appointment instructions.
- [ ] **6.** Check the issued documents, signatory and accurate identity details.

### Approved fellowship

- [ ] **1.** Match the exact fellowship to the current approved list.
- [ ] **2.** Confirm the talent or promise category attached to that fellowship.
- [ ] **3.** Obtain the official fellowship award letter.
- [ ] **4.** Check that the award dates meet the eligibility window.
- [ ] **5.** Check your name, awarding body and exact programme identity.
- [ ] **6.** Check the readable issued award letter against the current route instructions.

### UKRI funded role

- [ ] **1.** Verify the exact approved funder and UK host.
- [ ] **2.** Check qualifying grant type, at least £30,000 and at least two years.
- [ ] **3.** Check role, experience, at least half working time and one year remaining agreement.
- [ ] **4.** Obtain approved award database link or required funder letter.
- [ ] **5.** Obtain the authorised HR guarantee covering every required confirmation.
- [ ] **6.** Check all grants relied on and the current role and recruitment conditions.

### Peer review promise

- [ ] **1.** Confirm active research, approved discipline and PhD or equivalent experience.
- [ ] **2.** Check exceptional promise and early career standing.
- [ ] **3.** Prepare the typed research CV within three A4 pages.
- [ ] **4.** Obtain the main eligible UK resident eminent expert’s recommendation.
- [ ] **5.** Check letter contents, signature, date, credentials and contacts.
- [ ] **6.** Follow the selected academy’s form and any specific additional document instructions.

### Peer review talent

- [ ] **1.** Confirm active research, approved discipline, experience and exceptional standing.
- [ ] **2.** Prepare the typed research CV within three A4 pages.
- [ ] **3.** Obtain the main eligible UK resident eminent expert’s recommendation.
- [ ] **4.** Obtain the separate eligible senior UK expert’s objective assessment.
- [ ] **5.** Check both letters, independent assessment wording, credentials and contacts.
- [ ] **6.** Follow the selected academy’s form and any specific additional document instructions.


## Common follow-up items

These six source items apply after choosing the route, subject to the current official requirements.

- [ ] **1.** If needed, obtain required English translations or identity clarifications.
- [ ] **2.** Complete endorsement form, fee and required document handoff; save confirmation.
- [ ] **3.** Record endorsement decision and date; check the visa application deadline.
- [ ] **4.** Prepare passport, visa answers and any conditional visa documents.
- [ ] **5.** Complete visa payment and supporting document steps.
- [ ] **6.** Complete the assigned identity or biometrics process and monitor the decision.

## Personal progress log (keep private)

Use these columns in a private spreadsheet or notes file: **Date | What I did and the actual result | Evidence file link | My next step | Target date**. Each row should record one genuine action or outcome.

## Official sources

Confirm current requirements using the [GOV.UK Global Talent guidance](https://www.gov.uk/global-talent) and the relevant endorsement route. The source tracker may contain dated thresholds or procedural details and **does not replace live eligibility rules**.
