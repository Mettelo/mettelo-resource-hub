> **Use this as a PRIVATE working checklist:** GitHub displays these as unticked boxes for reading, but the publicly hosted document does **not** save your personal checkmarks. Copy the list into a private GitHub repository, notes app or tracker. Keep passport records, customer information, banking evidence and endorsement references private.

## Understand the checklist — what counts as a useful result?

The 20 source items are a **preparation sequence**, not 20 visa points. Do not mark a business task ready simply because a slide, website or unsigned letter exists.

| Workstream | Concrete activity worth completing | Evidence of actual progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Innovation** | Interview potential UK customers, compare three real alternatives and explain exactly what the proposed product does differently | Interview log, competitor matrix and specific original capability |
| **Founder credibility** | Identify each founder's original contribution, build responsibilities, skills and funding sources | Source-controlled implementation, authentic founder records and team skills |
| **Viability** | Prepare costs based on real supplier prices, delivery time, UK demand and actual accessible resources | Financial model with assumptions and pilot results |
| **Scalability** | Show testable customer acquisition, service operations, employment needs, costs and possible UK/national growth | Milestones, capacity estimates and defensible projections |
| **Endorsing body** | Find body on **current** GOV.UK authorised list and inspect its actual route, terms, cost and document requirements | Dated official eligibility review and submission record if genuinely made |
| **Visa stage** | Confirm English, maintenance, identity, endorsement letter and any conditional family/TB/translation requirements | Actual private documents and verified application instructions |

### Example: distinguish interest from demand

**Weak:** “More than 300 people joined our community, so 300 will pay for the product.”

**Better investigation:** Separate registrations, active users, enquiries, completed pilots and **actual paying customers**. Ask target buyers what they currently spend and what decision process would authorise a purchase. Capture price-testing limitations. A community can be a source of feedback; it does not automatically prove commercial viability.

### Three useful review questions

**Innovation:** If a competitor could reproduce the idea quickly, what technical, process or commercial advantage really remains?  
**Viability:** If first-year sales are half the forecast, what costs and commitments can the founders actually support?  
**Scalability:** What concrete operational changes, hiring, systems and funding would be needed before expansion, and what evidence supports those assumptions?

## Status, deadlines and evidence fields

The original Excel tracker uses five fields: **What I need to prepare**, **Status**, **Target date**, **Saved file link** and **Reminder and guide section**. The source also warns that a **Ready** status is your own document review, not a decision by an endorsing body or UKVI.

| What I need to prepare | Status | Target date | Saved file link | Reminder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Paste one task] | To do | YYYY-MM-DD | [Private evidence location] | [Outstanding details] |

Use **Not needed** only for a condition that demonstrably does not apply. Retain the actual evidence in an access-controlled location.

## Important

This checklist reproduces the workbook's practical task inventory; it is **not** a separate legal eligibility test and cannot guarantee endorsement, visa approval or settlement. Verify rules and required evidence against current [GOV.UK Innovator Founder guidance](https://www.gov.uk/innovator-founder-visa) and the relevant [authorised endorsing body](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/endorsing-bodies-innovator-founder-and-scale-up-visas/innovator-founder-and-scale-up-visas-endorsing-bodies).
