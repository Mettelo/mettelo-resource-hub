> **Who this guide is for:** Eligible adults renewing an existing Nigerian passport from the United Kingdom through the NIS contactless renewal process. The source excludes under-18s and first-time applicants. If you need to change personal details or replace a lost or damaged passport, check the appropriate appointment process instead.
>
> **Important:** This is an independent community guide, not an official Nigerian government service. Always confirm the current NIS instructions, payment screen and Nigeria High Commission requirements. The guide's source was checked 23 October 2026, and procedures may change.

## Start here: which renewal process actually applies to you?

**Critical current-rule check (9 November 2026):** The Nigeria Immigration Service publishes both a [general passport renewal process](https://immigration.gov.ng/info-center/renewal-of-passport/) that describes **in-person biometric appointments** and a [contactless passport service](https://immigration.gov.ng/contactless/) for qualifying cases. Therefore, **do not assume that every UK applicant can renew fully contactlessly or must post the same documents**. The eligibility and route shown in your actual official application portal, supported by the High Commission's instructions, govern your case.

| Your situation | First practical step | Do not do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adult renewing an existing passport with unchanged details | Check official renewal portal for current contactless eligibility and country/processing-centre availability | Do not buy postal orders or post your existing passport solely because an old guide mentions them |
| First Nigerian passport | Use [official NIS fresh application information](https://immigration.gov.ng/passports/) and relevant High Commission instructions | Do not follow an adult renewal-only workflow |
| Lost/damaged passport or changes to name/date/details | Use the correct [NIS replacement/data correction process](https://immigration.gov.ng/passports/) | Do not select ordinary renewal to bypass additional documents |
| Under-18 applicant | Check current minor requirements, parental consent and biometric route | Do not assume adult contactless eligibility |
| App payment/OTP/biometric error | Save official reference and seek NIS support through its current [contact page](https://immigration.gov.ng/contact/) or actual app help | Do not pay unofficial agents to “unlock” an application |

### Do this before paying or mailing anything

1. Open the [Nigeria Immigration Service](https://immigration.gov.ng/) and the **official** [passport portal](https://passport.immigration.gov.ng/), selecting the **outside Nigeria** application route when appropriate.
2. Choose the accurate application type. Check whether your exact case is eligible for mobile contactless capture or requires an appointment.
3. Confirm NIN/passport details, processing centre, payment method, approved documents, postage and address on the **live official instructions**.
4. Save the official application reference privately and track progress through the portal. Use app support for technical problems; the [NIS contactless incident notice](https://immigration.gov.ng/nis-addresses-technical-challenges-on-contactless-passport-application-system/) documents payment/OTP and technical challenges.
5. Protect personal documents and credentials. Never paste NIN, passport number, application codes, receipt images or home addresses into public GitHub issues or AI prompts.

**What changed compared with the original Mettelo handbook?** The source's 11-page process remains below as a reference for eligible contactless renewals, but **postal orders, envelopes, fees, app eligibility and physical appearance cannot be presumed current or universal**. Treat the official current portal as authoritative.

Confirm this directly before posting.

## 3. Complete the form and pay

*Original PDF page 3 of 11.*

STEPS 5 TO 7


Complete the form
and pay the booklet fee

5 Complete the application form
Enter your existing passport details and the information requested. Choose:

• United Kingdom as your application country.

• London or Nigeria High Commission as your processing mission.

• Your preferred passport booklet.

Complete your address and next of kin information. Upload your photo and the supporting
documents requested.

Photo: use a clear image against a plain, light background, facing straight ahead, with no
filters. The portal’s photo compliance check decides whether it is acceptable.

Review every section before submission, especially names, date of birth, email and
processing mission.


6 Pay the passport booklet fee
Pay through the official portal. Published overseas fees are: [3]

• 32 pages, valid for 5 years — US$150.

• 64 pages, valid for 10 years — US$230.

Bank or payment charges may apply. Confirm the displayed final total before paying. Some
official tables differ; rely on the current payment screen.

Save or download your completed application form, payment receipt, Application ID and
Reference Number. Keep both identifiers safe: you need them in the app.


7 Select the contactless option
Open “Application Status/Book Appointment”. Choose the contactless option, read the
instructions and confirm that you want to opt in. Button wording may change as the service
is updated. [2]

## 4. Download NIS Mobile and link the application

*Original PDF page 4 of 11.*

STEPS 8 AND 9


Download NIS Mobile
and link your application

8 Download the official NIS Mobile app
The app is called “NIS Mobile”. Check that the developer is Nigeria Immigration Service.
Download it only from your phone’s official app store.

Android download
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.irissmart.nismobile

iPhone download
https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/nis-mobile/id6749002693


9 Open the app and link your application
• Sign in or create an app profile as instructed.

• Complete any email verification requested.

• Select “Passport Application Services”.

• Then select “Passport Biometric Enrolment”.

• Enter your Application ID and Reference Number.

• Run the eligibility check. [2]

If the app says you are ineligible, return to the passport portal and arrange a physical
appointment. Do not create another paid application just to retry.


If your iPhone is not working
Practical tip from Johnson Taiwo’s experience: try the official app on an Android phone.
Android has worked better for him when iPhone capture failed. This is a useful fallback, not
an NIS requirement or a guaranteed fix.

Use a trusted device. Link the same application using your saved identifiers and sign out
afterwards if the phone is borrowed.

## 5. Capture face and fingerprints

*Original PDF page 5 of 11.*

STEPS 10 AND 11


Capture your face
and fingerprints

10 Capture your face
Allow camera access when requested. Before starting:

• Clean your camera lens and use good, even lighting.

• Keep your face fully visible and hold the phone steady.

• Position your face inside the on screen guide.

Follow the app’s instructions to capture your live facial image and complete the liveness
check. Liveness confirms a real person is present: perform whatever movements or actions
the app requests.


11 Capture your fingerprints
NIS Mobile uses your phone’s camera for this capture. You do not need to buy a fingerprint
scanner. Your phone’s normal fingerprint unlock feature does not replace this step.

• Make sure your fingers are clean and dry.

• Clean the camera lens.

• Keep the phone and your hand steady.

• Avoid shadows or glare across your fingertips.

Follow the hand and finger positions shown on screen. Move closer or further away only as
instructed. Repeat any capture the app rejects and complete all the fingers requested.

The app decides whether capture is acceptable. Do not rely on scores or techniques quoted
in someone else’s experience. It checks live facial images and fingerprints against NIS
records. [5]


If capture keeps failing
Check camera permissions and install any app update. Adjust lighting and positioning as
prompted. If using iPhone, try Android as explained on page 4. Contact NIS support if the
problem continues; avoid repeated new applications or payments.

12 Complete contactless payment and submission
Follow the app’s prompts to submit your biometrics and pay the separate contactless

NIS lists a US$100 contactless biometric service charge in addition to the passport booklet

The payment and submission order may vary with the app version. Complete every
required stage until the app confirms successful submission.

• Save the contactless biometric payment receipt.

• Save or screenshot the submission confirmation.

Finishing the website form alone does not complete contactless renewal. Check that your

13 Print your paperwork
Prepare printed copies of:

☐ Completed application form, signed and dated where required.

☐ Passport booklet payment receipt.

☐ Contactless biometric payment receipt.

☐ Additional confirmation or documents requested.

Keep copies of everything, including your old passport’s photo page, before posting.


Recommended covering note
Include your full name, Application ID, Reference Number, telephone, email and an item
list. This helps identify the package; it is not listed as a mandatory form.

A NIN slip copy and passport photo page copy are useful identification. Include additional
evidence requested by your application. Do not post other original certificates unless
instructed.

## 7. Buy postal order and return envelope

*Original PDF page 7 of 11.*

STEP 14


Buy your postal order
and return envelope

14 Visit the Post Office
Ask the counter staff for:

• A £20 postal order payable to “Nigeria High Commission, London”. Write your name and
   telephone number on its back. Keep the receipt. [1]

• A Royal Mail C4 Special Delivery envelope with return postage paid in advance. C4 holds
   A4 sheets without folding.

• A separate strong outer envelope large enough for the documents and empty return
   envelope.


What to say at the counter
“I am sending my passport to the Nigeria High Commission. I need a C4 Special Delivery
envelope with prepaid return postage, plus separate postage for my outward package.
Please check the return allowance covers my passport and documents.”


Set up the return envelope
☐ Write YOUR full name and UK delivery address on it.

☐ Make sure return postage is paid, not just the envelope.

☐ Confirm the service and weight allowance are suitable.

☐ Record the return tracking reference.

☐ Leave this envelope empty and unsealed.

Ask for postage suitable for return after passport processing. Check any label use by date;
do not assume a date specific online label remains usable weeks later.


If C4 envelopes are unavailable
Ask another branch or check the Royal Mail shop, which sells C4 Special Delivery envelopes
in packs of five. You need one return envelope for this package; confirm postage coverage
when buying. [7]

## 8. Pack and post documents

*Original PDF page 8 of 11.*

STEP 15


Pack and post
your documents to London

15 Assemble the package
Only post after successful contactless biometric enrolment. Put these inside the OUTER
envelope:

☐ Original old Nigerian passport booklet.

☐ Printed, completed application form.

☐ Booklet payment and contactless payment receipts.

☐ £20 postal order.

☐ Empty, unsealed, prepaid C4 return envelope addressed to you.

☐ Any other documents specifically requested.


    OUTER ENVELOPE • TO LONDON

    Nigeria High Commission
    9 Northumberland Avenue
    London WC2N 5BX
    United Kingdom

    RETURN ENVELOPE • BACK TO YOU

    Your full name
    Your house number and street
    Your town or city
    Your UK postcode


Place the documents neatly together. Put your sender address on the back of the outer
envelope, then seal it. Keep barcode labels visible. The return envelope goes inside; do not
seal it around the documents.

Ask the Post Office to weigh and send the outer package by Special Delivery. Pay the
separate outward postage and keep proof of posting. Hand it in at the counter, not a street
postbox. [8]

Record BOTH tracking references: outward delivery to London and return delivery to you.
Keep secure copies of the papers, passport photo page and postal order. [1]

## Important source-versus-official differences

### Passport fees

The source guide lists **US$150 for a 32-page, five-year booklet**, **US$230 for a 64-page, ten-year booklet**, and a **US$100 contactless biometric fee**. An NIS service-level document currently available online lists a **US$130 32-page fee** for certain contactless service entries, while other published fee tables may differ. **Do not rely on either historical printed amount as your final payable price.** The live official portal's payment screen governs the amount quoted for your actual application, and any separate service charge must be confirmed there. Do not pay through unofficial agents.

### Contactless versus appointment routes

The original guide covers **contactless renewal** only. The standard NIS renewal information still describes appointment-based biometric capture, and the High Commission states that first-time applicants and children under 18 cannot use its contactless route. If NIS Mobile declares your case ineligible, follow the appointment route applicable to your application; do not create duplicate paid applications.

### Postal package

The High Commission currently lists the **£20 administrative postal order**, a **Royal Mail C4 Special Delivery self-addressed return envelope**, the old passport if applicable, and application forms/payment receipt. This official list is more authoritative than supplementary suggestions in the original guide. Always verify where and how documents should be sent before posting your passport.

### Technology and support

The original guide recommends Android as a practical fallback if NIS Mobile facial/biometric capture fails on iPhone. That is an individual experience-based tip, **not a mandatory NIS rule or guaranteed fix**. Use only the official NIS Mobile app and contact NIS support for unresolved problems.

### Privacy and safety

Do not post your passport number, NIN, application ID, reference number, identity images, home address or payment details in public GitHub issues or discussion threads. Keep your receipts, evidence and return-tracking information in a secure private location.
