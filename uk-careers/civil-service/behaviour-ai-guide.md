## Original page 1

Civil Service Behaviour (250-word STAR) AI Guide
Purpose

This guide helps you generate a strong, submission-ready 250-word STAR response for a Civil Service
behaviour using AI. Behaviour responses are strictly assessed and must demonstrate ownership, judgement,
complexity, and measurable impact at the required grade.


           Civil Service Behaviour (250-word STAR) Prompt
Use this after pasting the job description, behaviour definition, grade, and your CV.

“Using my current CV together with the full job description and the specific Civil Service behaviour being
assessed, identify the strongest and most relevant example from my experience that best demonstrates this
behaviour at the required grade. Select an example showing clear ownership, complexity, decision-making
responsibility, and measurable impact. Then write a single STAR response of approximately 250 words aligned
directly to the behaviour. Apply the Blind CV rule by not naming specific employers or organisations and
instead using sector-based descriptors only (such as Central Government Department or Arm’s Length Public
Body). Structure the response clearly into Situation, Task, Action, and Result, ensuring the Action phase is the
most detailed section. The Action must explain the specific steps I personally took, decisions made, how I
prioritised competing demands, managed risks, engaged stakeholders, applied tools or frameworks, and
ensured quality and timely delivery. The Result must demonstrate tangible or measurable impact where
credible. Use professional British English with a confident, authentic tone. The final output must be concise,
evidence-based, and submission-ready.”

How to Use
   1. Open a new chat in ChatGPT (or similar tool).
   2. Paste in this order:

               •   Job Description
               •   Behaviour definition
               •   Grade level
               •   Your full CV
               •   The prompt above
   3. Submit and review carefully.

What to Check Before Submitting
   •   The example directly reflects the named behaviour.

## Original page 2

• The Action section is detailed and clearly shows what you did.

   •   There is evidence of ownership and judgement.

   •   The Result shows measurable or meaningful impact.

   •   No employer names appear (Blind rule).

Word Limit Reminder
Civil Service behaviour responses are typically capped at 250 words. Keep the Situation brief, limit background
detail, focus heavily on Action, and finish with a strong impact-led Result.

Disclaimer (100 Words)

AI-generated behaviour responses are drafting tools and must be reviewed before submission. While AI can
structure content using the STAR format and align examples to the behaviour definition, it may oversimplify
context or misrepresent nuance. You are responsible for ensuring all examples are accurate, truthful, and
genuinely reflective of your experience. Before submitting, refine the Action section to ensure clear personal
ownership, strengthen measurable impact, and adjust the tone to reflect your authentic professional voice.
The final submission must demonstrate judgement, credibility, and integrity, not solely automated drafting.

## Before you apply

- Check the specific job advert for word limits, assessed criteria, anonymity and permitted AI assistance.
- Validate each claim, tool, date, responsibility and outcome against your own evidence.
- Never submit a prompt response without personal editing and factual checking.
- Use this resource for preparation; it does not guarantee shortlisting or an interview.
