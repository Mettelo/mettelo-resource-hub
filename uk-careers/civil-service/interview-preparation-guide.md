## Original page 1

Interview Preparation Guide (Civil Service & NHS)
Purpose
This guide helps you prepare structured, high-scoring interview responses for Civil Service and NHS roles
using AI as a preparation tool. Public-sector interviews are competency- and values-based. Answers must
demonstrate evidence, ownership, impact, and alignment to behaviours or values.

Civil Service Interview Preparation Prompt (Blind)
Use this after pasting the job description, behaviours, and your CV.

“Using my current CV together with the full job description, person specification, Civil Service behaviours,
strengths profile (if applicable), and any listed technical or experience-based requirements, identify the likely
interview questions that could be asked across Behaviour, Strength, Technical, and Experience-based areas at
the stated grade. For each assessed area, review my CV and identify at least two highly relevant examples, each
with a concise justification explaining why it is strong and aligned with the requirement. Then, for each
Behaviour question, select the strongest relevant example and draft a structured STAR response suitable for
verbal delivery (approximately 2–3 minutes). Ensure the Action phase is detailed, clearly explaining the steps I
personally took, decisions made, stakeholder engagement, prioritisation, risk management, and any tools,
frameworks, or methods applied. Include measurable or tangible impact in the Result. Apply the Blind rule by
not naming specific employers; instead, use sector-based descriptors where necessary. Use professional British
English with a natural, confident tone appropriate for spoken delivery, ensuring responses are clear, structured,
and credible rather than scripted or overly formal.”

NHS Interview Preparation Prompt
NHS interviews are not blind; they retain organisation and Trust names.

“Using my current CV together with the job description, person specification, and Trust values, generate likely
interview questions aligned to the essential criteria and NHS values. For each question, select the strongest
relevant example and draft a clear STAR response suitable for verbal delivery (2–3 minutes). The Action phase
must be detailed, explaining my personal contribution, collaboration with multidisciplinary teams, governance
awareness, safeguarding considerations, and service improvement methods used. Demonstrate measurable
impact on patient care, service delivery, compliance, or operational performance where credible. Ensure
responses reflect NHS values through evidence, not slogans.”

How to Strengthen Interview Answers
       •   Keep Situation concise.
       •   Focus heavily on detailed Action.
       •   Demonstrate ownership and judgement.
       •   Quantify results where possible.
       •   Show reflection: what you learned and how you improved.
       •   Adapt examples naturally rather than memorising scripts.

Interview Success Tips
       •   Understand the behaviour or value being assessed.
       •   Prepare 5–6 strong examples that can flex across questions.
       •   Structure answers clearly using STAR.
       •   Maintain professional, calm, and confident delivery.
       •   Be authentic; interviewers value credibility over perfection.

Disclaimer
AI-generated interview answers are preparation tools and must not be memorised verbatim. While AI can
help structure strong STAR responses and anticipate likely questions, it cannot replicate your authentic
delivery or professional judgement. You are responsible for ensuring all examples are accurate, truthful, and
genuinely reflective of your experience. Before interview, refine responses, simplify overly complex phrasing,
and practise delivering answers naturally. The strongest interviews demonstrate clarity, ownership,
measurable impact, and reflective learning. Use AI to structure preparation, but rely on your own
understanding, confidence, and integrity during the interview.

## Before you apply

- Check the specific job advert for word limits, assessed criteria, anonymity and permitted AI assistance.
- Validate each claim, tool, date, responsibility and outcome against your own evidence.
- Never submit a prompt response without personal editing and factual checking.
- Use this resource for preparation; it does not guarantee shortlisting or an interview.
